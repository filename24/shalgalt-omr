# shalgalt-omr — User Manual [#shalgalt-omr--user-manual]

**shalgalt-omr** reads and grades OMR (fill-the-bubble) answer sheets automatically,
**fully offline**, on your own computer. As a teacher you can:

1. **Design a template** — draw the answer-sheet layout in a visual editor.
2. **Generate a printable PDF** of that template.
3. **Grade** scanned or phone-photographed sheets automatically.
4. **Review** low-confidence sheets by hand.
5. **Export** results to Excel, or share a whole project as a `.shalgalt` file.

> The interface of the application itself is in **Mongolian only**. This English manual is a
> translation of the Mongolian user guide; some pages may fall back to Mongolian until their
> translation is added.

## Privacy [#privacy]

The app needs **no internet** and sends **nothing** anywhere. Student data stays on your
computer. See the [Privacy statement](/user/privacy).
